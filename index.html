Our philosophy

Teeth affect how you eat, speak and feel in a room full of people. Tooth loss can also change your face shape and your bone over time. That is why we treat implants as a long term health decision as well as a cosmetic one. Our goal is a result that functions well, looks natural and lasts.

Complex cases and second opinions

Many patients come to us after being told they lack bone or that implants will not work. We handle complex dental implant cases with bone grafting, sinus procedures and zygomatic implants when needed. We also offer a second opinion for anyone unsure about a treatment plan.

If an implant has failed, we find out why. Infection, bite forces, positioning and healing all play a role. Our dental implant rescue process removes the problem, rebuilds where necessary and replaces the implant with a stronger plan.

Cosmetic and aesthetic care

Strong implants should also look beautiful. Our smile design and aesthetic dentistry work covers tooth shape, gum line and color so your smile transformation looks like you, only better.

Frequently asked questions

Are dental implants permanent?

The titanium implant is designed to last for many years when you keep your gums healthy and attend regular visits. The visible crown or bridge may need maintenance over time.

What is All On 4?

All On 4 uses four implants to support a full arch of fixed teeth. It is a popular permanent teeth solution for patients with extensive tooth loss.

Can I get teeth in one day?

Many patients qualify for same day dental implants with temporary teeth placed at surgery. Your exam and imaging confirm whether this is right for you.

Is implant surgery painful?

Most patients report manageable soreness. We use local anesthesia, and sedation options are available for comfort.

Can failed implants be fixed?

Often yes. We evaluate the cause and offer revision or replacement when it is the safest choice.
